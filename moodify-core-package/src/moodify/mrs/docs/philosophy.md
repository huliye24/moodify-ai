# MRS Philosophy

## Why Audio Evaluation Matters in the AI Era

Historically, music creation and evaluation have been coupled through human roles:

```text
Human producer + Human engineer
        ↓
Creation + Evaluation
```

AI generation changes the rate at which audio can be produced, but generation alone does not establish whether an output is technically sound, suitable for a stated listening context, or preferred by people.

```text
AI generation
        ↓
AI understanding + AI evaluation
```

Generation without evaluation is incomplete.

MRS is a research attempt to make evaluation more explicit and testable. It begins with observable acoustic evidence, retains the need for human listening judgment, and treats preference learning as future work requiring consented data, task definition, calibration, and uncertainty handling.

## Design Commitments

- A score must state what it measures and what it does not measure.
- Signal metrics are inputs to evaluation, not proof of artistic value.
- A machine must defer when its evidence or authority is insufficient.
- Human preference is contextual; it cannot be inferred as fact from a technical score alone.
