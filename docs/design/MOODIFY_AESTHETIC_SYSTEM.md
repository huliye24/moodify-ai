# Moodify Aesthetic System v0.1

> The Ear of AI - an auditory instrument with quiet authority.

This is the canonical product-design interpretation of *Moodify Aesthetic
Principles v0.1*. Aesthetic quality is not a decorative pass. It is the visible
result of technical, informational, and operational order.

## Product posture

Moodify is an auditory instrument, not a music feed and not a preset console.
Its primary surface makes the listening state, current judgment, evidence
quality, and next human decision immediately legible. Technical depth remains
available, but appears progressively.

## The six layers

1. **Form** - restrained proportions, decisive alignment, limited curvature.
2. **Material** - near-black graphite, mineral surfaces, hairline boundaries.
3. **Acoustics** - visual rhythm reflects precision, dynamics, and low fatigue.
4. **Human** - large targets, calm density, low cognitive and visual pressure.
5. **Interaction** - state changes are quiet, short, reversible, and explicit.
6. **Spirit** - controlled, exact, durable, authoritative without spectacle.

## Visual grammar

- **Field:** graphite rather than pure black; warm mineral white for text.
- **Signal:** one desaturated mineral green denotes active evidence or progress.
- **Alert:** amber is reserved for human attention; red only for blocking failure.
- **Depth:** hierarchy comes from luminance, spacing, and one-pixel borders.
- **Shape:** 8/12/16 dp radii. Pills are reserved for compact state labels.
- **Type:** modest weights, generous line height, tabular numerals for evidence.
- **Motion:** no bounce or ornamental loops; reveal complexity in one direction.
- **Data:** charts answer one question each. Parameters support a judgment.

## First-layer information order

1. Identity: `MOODIFY / THE EAR OF AI`
2. Instrument state: ready, listening, judging, waiting for human, or blocked
3. Primary action: introduce one source to the listening loop
4. Canonical loop: Listen -> Represent -> Judge -> Intervene -> Verify -> Learn
5. Recent evidence: cases and their authority state, never engagement metrics

## Rejection rules

Reject an element when it does not improve hearing, understanding, action,
evidence, or product character. In particular, avoid decorative gradients,
feed-like popularity metrics, generic creator-social surfaces, excessive card
shadows, competing accent colors, and technical density without a decision.

## Review gate

Every new surface must answer:

- What stage of the auditory loop does this serve?
- What is the single dominant decision?
- What evidence is visible now, and what is progressively disclosed?
- What happens when evidence is absent, uncertain, or failed?
- Would the surface still feel coherent without its logo?
- Is it likely to remain credible in ten years?
