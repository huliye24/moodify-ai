## Content Narrative

### Problem Statement

> "Music entered the streaming era. Playback stayed in the fixed era."

The same audio plays the same way on different devices. The same volume for quiet and loud tracks. This is not wrong, it is incomplete.

### Solution Statement

> "Moodify listens before it plays."

Analyze the audio signal first. Understand dynamics, spectrum, phase, structure. Then determine how this specific track can sound best.

### Research Question

> "Can machines learn to hear?"

Not just process audio. Understand what happens in sound. This is the frontier Moodify explores.

### Philosophy

> "Music is an experience, not just a file."

Availability is not the same as experience. Technology should adapt to humans, not the other way around.

## Brand Voice

### What We Use
- Direct statements
- Questions that invite thinking
- Specific claims with stated limitations
- Technical precision without jargon
- Respect for the listener

### What We Avoid
- Marketing superlatives ("revolutionary", "game-changing")
- Vague promises ("better experience" without explanation)
- Feature lists without purpose
- Hype about AI
- Claims about competitors
