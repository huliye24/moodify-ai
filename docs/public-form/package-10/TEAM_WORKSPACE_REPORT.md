## Compensation & Runway Principles

### Current Stage (Pre-revenue)

| Role | Compensation Approach | Range |
|---|---|---|
| Founder | Equity only (initially) | 100% ownership |
| First hire | Below-market cash + meaningful equity | ¥15-25K/mo + 1-3% |
| Advisor | Equity only | 0.1-0.5% |

### Rules

1. **Don't outrun runway** — Total compensation < 20% of remaining runway per month
2. **Equity means ownership** — Early employees are partners, not contractors
3. **Vesting standard** — 4-year vesting, 1-year cliff for significant grants
4. **No phantom equity** — If someone isn't contributing, don't give equity for "potential"
5. **Market transparency** — Tell candidates honestly about stage and risk

## Co-location Decision Framework

### Options

| Option | Pros | Cons | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Remote-first** | Talent anywhere, lower cost | Coordination harder, culture challenge | Default recommendation |
| **Same city** | Easier collaboration, serendipity | Higher cost, smaller talent pool | If team < 3 and wants intensity |
| **Hybrid** | Flexibility + some face time | Complex logistics | Post-COVID norm if done well |
| **Co-working** | Professional space, low commitment | Noise, distractions | Early stage, no office lease |

### Recommendation for Moodify

**Start remote-first.** Reasons:
1. Single founder can hire globally
2. Lower burn (critical pre-revenue)
3. Async-friendly workflow already established (package system)
4. Audio product doesn't require physical lab

**Consider co-location when:**
- Team reaches 3+ people who want it
- Creative collaboration benefits from in-person
- Investor/board prefers visible team

## 30-60-90 Transition Plan (For New Hires)

### First 30 Days: Learn & Small Wins

- Week 1: Environment setup, code access, read key docs
- Week 2: First small PR/bug fix (supervised)
- Week 3: First feature contribution (paired)
- Week 4: Own a small area independently

**Goal:** New hire productive but not overwhelmed.

### Days 31-60: Ownership & Integration

- Take ownership of 1 defined area
- Participate in planning/review sessions
- Build relationships with founder/advisors
- Understand product vision deeply (read P01-P05)

**Goal:** New hire is a contributor, not just a worker.

### Days 61-90: Autonomy & Initiative

- Propose improvements (not just implement)
- Mentor next new hire (if applicable)
- Represent company externally (if appropriate)
- Full team member in decision discussions

**Goal:** New hire is a partner, not an employee.

## Team Culture & Working Norms (Draft)

### Values (Suggested)

1. **Listen first** — Before arguing, understand
2. **Evidence over opinion** — Show your work
3. **Respect the belief** — "每一种声音" applies internally too
4. **Own your output** — If you made it, stand by it
5. **Ask for help early** — Stuck > 30 min = ask

### Communication Norms

| Practice | Rule |
|---|---|
| Async by default | Don't require synchronous unless urgent |
| Write it down | If it's not written, it didn't happen |
| Default public | Internal docs default to team-visible |
| Meeting cost | Every meeting needs agenda + outcome |
| Feedback direct | Give it kindly, receive it gratefully |

### Working Hours

- **No core hours requirement** (async-friendly)
- **Response time expectation:** Same business day for non-urgent
- **Urgency definition:** User-facing outage / investor deadline = urgent
- **Time off:** Unlimited policy with responsibility (common for startups)
