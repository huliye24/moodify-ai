# Visual Interpretation Limits

Frequency plots show measured energy over time and frequency. They can reveal bandwidth, silence, transients, noise patterns and processing differences under frozen parameters.

They cannot by themselves establish musical quality, emotional truth, instrument identity, listener preference, mastering superiority or release readiness. Brightness, smoothness and visual density are not optimization targets.

Before and after plots use the same absolute amplitude reference, FFT/window/hop, analysis sample rate, channel policy and fixed color limits. The difference plot is `after_db - before_db`; positive means measured energy increased. Human listening remains independent evidence.
