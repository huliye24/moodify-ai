# Artistic Identity Policy

**Part of:** [CLASSIC_RECONSTRUCTION_CONSTITUTION.md](CLASSIC_RECONSTRUCTION_CONSTITUTION.md) (v1.0)
**Status:** LIVE

---

## 1. Doctrine: Preserve Identity Before Improve Sound

Any future processing must satisfy all six requirements:

1. the improvement target is explicit;
2. the processing reason is explainable;
3. before/after is verifiable;
4. core artistic identity is not damaged;
5. failure can roll back;
6. uncertainty may bypass.

If any requirement cannot be met, the intervention does not proceed.

## 2. No Universal Modernization

Forbidden as a default or universal outcome:

- all tracks louder;
- all tracks brighter;
- all tracks wider;
- all tracks cleaner;
- all tracks more compressed;
- all bass heavier;
- all vocals more forward.

## 3. Presets Are Candidates, Not Truth

`clean_master` and every other preset:

- may exist;
- are **intervention candidates**（候选干预）;
- are never the default product answer;
- are never treated as truth.

A preset becomes a candidate only through the decision model
([RECONSTRUCTION_BOUNDARIES.md](RECONSTRUCTION_BOUNDARIES.md)) — it never bypasses it.

## 4. Human Artistic Authority

Machines may detect, estimate, rank and propose.

Machines do not automatically possess final artistic authority.

When the machine cannot safely separate artistic choice from technical limitation, the decision belongs to a human reviewer, recorded with reviewer, scope, time and supporting evidence — consistent with root `AGENTS.md` judgment authority.

## 5. North Star Check

Every reconstruction algorithm, before entering production, must answer:

> **Does this make the song better to hear without making it less itself?**

它是否让这首歌更好听，同时仍然是它自己？

A capability that cannot answer this question cannot automatically enter production.
