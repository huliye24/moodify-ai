## Whitepaper acceptance invariants

- `WORLD + PROTOCOL + PORTAL` resolves to one coherent network state.
- Wallet address alone is not treated as a complete identity.
- Reputation is explainable and derived from verifiable contribution history, not purchased balance.
- Agents and nodes must prove work or service; registration alone is insufficient.
- Network health is measured by real residents, contributions, agents, nodes, applications, MIPs, and treasury facts, not price alone.
- A real count of one is preferred to a fictional global-scale count.
- Public token/tax/treasury claims must match deployed contracts and observable chain state.
- Experimental or early-network limitations remain visible.
