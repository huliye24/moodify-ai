## WHAT MOOD IS

MOOD v0.1 is an open digital world and protocol network with one public interface:

```text
MOOD
  -> crestwavecoin.com
     -> WORLD       experience and orientation
     -> PROTOCOL    public, versioned, verifiable rules
     -> PORTAL      real participation
        -> Wallet / Passport
        -> Contribution / Proof / Reputation
        -> Agents / Nodes
        -> Governance
     -> NETWORK     source-backed current state
     -> Moodify     first Genesis Application
```

Moodify Protocol defines sound, AI, contribution, proof, Agent, Node, governance, economics, and security rules required by this network. MOOD Token may become an economic medium for settlement, incentive, staking, and governance; it is not MOOD itself.

## WHAT MOOD IS NOT

MOOD v0.1 is not:

- a token-only project, meme coin, price narrative, or fundraising promise;
- an exchange, broker, custodian, investment product, or guaranteed-return system;
- a collection of unrelated AI products or experimental workspaces;
- an Electron workspace, plugin marketplace, enterprise security suite, benchmark product, generic SDK business, or commercial SaaS bundle;
- a second audio suite, mastering product, DSP preset marketplace, or public Ear product;
- a dashboard of invented residents, nodes, agents, contributions, liquidity, revenue, or global scale;
- an excuse to expose private keys, private infrastructure, private audio, or participant data;
- a license to bypass human authority over contracts, funds, treasury, governance, security, legal review, or production deployment.

Historical work may remain as evidence. It does not acquire mainline authority merely because it exists or passes isolated tests.

| Treatment | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `KEEP` | Required by the whitepaper's minimum real network and supported by tests/evidence |
| `FREEZE` | Potentially useful later; no current development or mainline expansion |

`DELETE` is a classification before it is a filesystem action. No material deletion occurs without exact targets, evidence, migration/rollback, and review.
