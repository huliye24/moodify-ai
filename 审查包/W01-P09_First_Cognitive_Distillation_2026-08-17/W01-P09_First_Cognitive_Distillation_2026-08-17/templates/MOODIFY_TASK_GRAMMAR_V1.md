# Moodify Task Grammar v1

Future task packages should normally answer these nine slots.

## 1. Intent
What single problem does this task resolve?

## 2. Reality Gate
What must be observed/verified before action?

## 3. Authority
Who/what has the right to decide?

## 4. Inputs
What exact artifacts/contracts enter?

## 5. Scope
What may change and what is forbidden?

## 6. Execution
What is the minimum sequence?

## 7. Evidence
What proves the work happened?

## 8. Acceptance
What exact conditions mean done?

## 9. Handoff
What does the next task receive?

## Compression Rule

Do not copy all Wave 01 templates into every future task.

Use only the minimum structure required by risk and novelty.
