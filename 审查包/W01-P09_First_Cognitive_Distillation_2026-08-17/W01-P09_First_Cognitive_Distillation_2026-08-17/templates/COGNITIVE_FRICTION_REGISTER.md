# Cognitive Friction Register

## Necessary Friction

| ID | Friction | Why Necessary | Evidence | Preserve As |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Examples:

- rights review
- destructive migration review
- security authorization
- human listening judgment where uncertainty remains

## Avoidable Friction

| ID | Friction | Occurrences | Evidence | Current Cost | Distillation Target | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Examples:

- repeated context lookup
- repeated deployment identity checks
- repeated manual evidence stitching
- repeated known-failure diagnosis

## Principle

Low friction means fewer repeated payments, not less careful thinking.
