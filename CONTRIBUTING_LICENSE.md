# Contribution Licensing

By submitting a contribution for inclusion in a part of Moodify marked
`GPL-3.0-only`, the contributor agrees that the contribution may be distributed
under GNU GPL Version 3 only, unless a separate written agreement applies.

The contributor represents that:

1. the contribution is original or the contributor has sufficient rights to
   submit it;
2. third-party code and assets are clearly identified with their licenses;
3. no confidential information, credentials, customer material, or restricted
   dataset is included;
4. known patent or licensing constraints relevant to the contribution are
   disclosed.

This file is a basic inbound-licensing rule. It is not a substitute for a
Contributor License Agreement when the copyright owner intends to relicense,
dual-license, or sell proprietary exceptions for community contributions.
