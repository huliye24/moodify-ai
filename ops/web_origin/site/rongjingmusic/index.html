THE QUESTION

Generation is not hearing

A generative model may produce audio without reliably answering: what happened in the waveform, spectrum, dynamics, phase, or structure? What is measurable and what remains uncertain? If a change was made, did it achieve the intended result? Can evidence from this case improve the next one?
