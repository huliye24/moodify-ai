The Problem

Music entered the streaming era.
Playback stayed in the fixed era.

Every song is different. Every device is different. Every listener is different. Yet playback treats them all the same.

The same audio file plays the same way on headphones, speakers, and phones. The same volume level for quiet acoustic tracks and loud electronic productions. The same signal path regardless of the room, the time, or the listener.

This is not a complaint about streaming services. It is a recognition that playback can be better.

The Approach

Moodify uses AI audio technology to find better ways to play each song.

Not by changing the music. By understanding it first.

Moodify listens before it plays. It analyzes the audio signal — dynamics, spectrum, phase, structure — and determines how that specific track can sound best on that specific device, for that specific listener.

The complexity stays inside. The experience stays simple: press Play, hear better.

Research

Can machines learn to hear?

This is the question Moodify exists to explore.

Humans developed visual intelligence — the ability to recognize, understand, and interpret images. We believe machines need auditory intelligence: the ability to understand what happens in sound, not just process it.

Our research explores:

  • Audio Understanding — What is happening in this sound?
  • Signal Analysis — What can we measure and how reliably?
  • Perceptual Evaluation — What do listeners actually prefer?
  • Playback Intelligence — How should this sound be played?
