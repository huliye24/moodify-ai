Privacy — Phase I statement

This site is static: it collects no accounts, no analytics beacons, and no private audio. What we commit to across the product family is below.

This websiteNo cookies, no analytics scripts, no tracking. Standard server logs only.
Private audioEar sources and evidence are private by default; only publish-safe, approved evidence is ever displayed.
Music accountsSessions are server-issued, revocable, and never exposed in logs. Drafts and inbox are private to the owner.
Never collectedWe do not collect private audio bodies, prompts, or judgment logs into analytics; logs are redacted of tokens, cookies, invite codes, and private paths.
RetentionBackups follow declared retention; deletion requests are honored with audit, and automated deletion is not performed silently.
ContactPrivacy questions: hello@rongjingmusic.com

Effective 2026-08-14. This statement accompanies the Phase I launch; it will be revised as capabilities evolve.
