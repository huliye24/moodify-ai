INTERNAL CAPABILITY · HISTORICAL EXPLANATION

Auditory judgment
with traceable evidence

This page explains Moodify's internal auditory system. Moodify Ear is not a public consumer product; the public product is Moodify Music.

Ear serves the master loop — Listen → Represent → Judge → Intervene → Verify → Learn — for researchers, producers, AI audio teams, and designated reviewers.

Moodify Ear is not an automatic-mastering product, a preset browser, a generic audio editor, or a black-box quality score.

THE CASE

One sound, one case, one accountable loop

Introduce sourceFilename, duration, format, sample rate, channels, SHA-256 identity, and an explicit analysis objective.
RepresentComplementary views across waveform, spectrum, loudness, dynamics, phase, and structure. What cannot be measured is stated as unavailable.
JudgeFindings are specific and inspectable: what was detected, why it matters, which evidence supports it, how confident we are, what could invalidate it.
InterveneOptional, justified by a finding, and recorded with rationale, parameters, engine version, and intended target. No intervention is a valid outcome.
VerifyBefore/after comparison against the declared objective, separating measured change from preference from unresolved uncertainty.
LearnEligible cases update benchmarks, calibration, failure catalogues, and — after governance — versioned Rules. One success does not create a rule.

AUTHORITY

Automation never manufactures certainty: unresolved perceptual cases escalate or close as inconclusive, and technical ranking is never presented as artistic quality or copyright conclusion.
