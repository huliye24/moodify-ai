ABOUT

Can machines learn to hear?

Moodify exists because generating sound and hearing sound are different capabilities. A system that produces audio is not thereby able to understand what happened, judge it responsibly, verify an intervention, or learn from evidence.

THESIS

The Ear of AI

Moodify is an auditory intelligence system. Its canonical loop — Listen → Represent → Judge → Intervene → Verify → Learn — turns listening into an inspectable, repeatable engineering and research problem.

WSEWhat happened in the sound — waveform, spectrum, loudness, dynamics, phase.
MSEWhat is the musical structure — beat, tempo, sections, roles.
PPEHow the result is produced, verified, and recovered reliably.

PRINCIPLES
