MOODIFY MUSIC

Moodify listens before you do.

A player that listens to music before it plays music. The public axis is simple: Library → Track → Now Playing → Play. The listening and preparation stay inside Moodify.

LISTENER

Library · Track · Now Playing · Play

LibraryFavorites and recent listening. Capabilities reflect real server state — unavailable actions are visibly disabled.
TrackOne public track page with credits, version context, and an optional Creation Passport summary.
Now PlayingStable playback with an understandable player and honest platform limits. No autoplay pressure.
PlayPlayback is the point. Moodify keeps the listening, judgment and preparation inside — the public experience stays simple.

Following, support and licensing are subordinate capabilities — intent is recorded, never treated as payment or legal grant.

CREATOR

Create · Version · Declare provenance · Publish · Reach · Receive opportunities

Immutable versionsA public work can evolve without erasing its history. Versions are never overwritten in place.
Creation PassportA creator's provenance declaration with an explicit boundary statement.
Recoverable publishingInterrupted upload or lost publish responses are recovered from authoritative server state — never by guessing.
OwnershipOnly authorized owners can modify or publish. Client-supplied identity is never authoritative.

The Creation Passport is a creator declaration. It is not copyright certification, legal title, or automatic Moodify Ear approval.

BOUNDED INTELLIGENCE

Ear evidence stays subordinate to music

When offered, Ear analysis appears as a bounded, publish-safe evidence section. Experimental metrics never become public rankings or quality certification, and a track's publication state is never decided by an Ear job.
