Process

Five stages of audio intelligence

01

Input

Original audio

The process begins with the audio file as provided. No preprocessing. No assumptions about format, genre, or quality.

We accept what we are given and understand it on its own terms.

02

Analysis

Audio feature extraction

The system analyzes the audio signal across multiple dimensions:

  • Temporal — dynamics, transients, envelope
  • Spectral — frequency content, balance, texture
  • Spatial — stereo field, phase relationships
  • Structural — sections, transitions, patterns

What cannot be measured is noted as unavailable.

03

Processing

AI audio enhancement

Based on the analysis, the system determines what processing would benefit this specific track:

  • Dynamic range adaptation
  • Spectral balance adjustment
  • Spatial field optimization
  • Temporal coherence enhancement

No preset is applied blindly. Each decision is specific to this audio.

04

Optimization

Playback adaptation

The processed audio is then adapted for the specific playback context:

  • Device — headphones, speakers, phone
  • Environment — quiet room, commute, outdoors
  • Listener — personal preferences, hearing profile
  • Content — genre, production style, intent

The same track may be optimized differently for different contexts.

05

Output

Better listening experience

The result is audio that sounds better on this device, in this environment, for this listener — without changing the music itself.

Press Play. Hear the difference.

Principles

How we build

Hearing before intervention

We analyze first. We understand what is in the audio before deciding what to do with it.

Evidence before claims

We verify. Every processing decision must be inspectable and reproducible.

Specificity before generality

We treat each track individually. No presets applied blindly.

Transparency before mystery

We state what we know, what we don't know, and what we are uncertain about.
